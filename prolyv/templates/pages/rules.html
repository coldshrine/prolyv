Community rules

Prolyv is for people who like tea and like talking about it. The rules are short.

Be decent

Disagree about the tea, not the person. No harassment, hate or threats. Everyone was a beginner once, so beginner questions are welcome.

Be honest

Say what you actually brewed and tasted. If you sell tea or work for someone who does, say so when you mention them. No hidden advertising and no spam.

No health promises

Share your experience, but don't tell people a tea will treat an illness. Medical questions belong with a doctor.

Respect other people's work

Only post text and photos you have the right to share, and credit your sources.

Moderation

Anyone can press Report. Admins may hide content or restrict an account that breaks these rules.
